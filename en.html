7. Description of Services Offered by Rook

Rook provides an information aggregation and territorial promotion platform designed to connect visitors and event organizers in Tuscany:

Event Catalog & Search

Browse, search, and filter food festivals, cultural celebrations, music events, and fairs across all 10 provinces of Tuscany.

Interactive Map & Geolocation

View event locations on an interactive map powered by Google Maps SDK, with real-time distance calculations based on temporary device GPS.

Favorites & Keyword Alerts

Bookmark favorite events, follow favorite organizers, and set custom keyword alerts to receive instant notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Ratings & Reviews

Registered users can submit ratings and reviews to share feedback on attended events, with full authority to modify or delete their reviews anytime.

In-App Direct Chat

Direct messaging between visitors and event organizers to request logistical, dietary, or scheduling information.

Organizer Tools & Boosts

Dedicated tools for verified organizers to publish events, manage staff permissions, initiate inter-entity collaborations, and request visibility boosts.
